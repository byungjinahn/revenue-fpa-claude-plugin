# ASC 606 notes for consumption models

Planning notes only. Not accounting advice; confirm treatment with Revenue Accounting.

- **Committed minimums (stand-ready obligation).** A minimum monthly or annual commit is often recognized ratably over the period. Usage above the commit (overage) is typically recognized as consumed.
- **Prepaid drawdown and breakage.** When customers prepay units that may go unused, expected breakage can be recognized in proportion to the pattern of usage if it can be reasonably estimated and is not expected to reverse. Otherwise, recognize breakage when the likelihood of use becomes remote. Shortfall flags in the model are inputs to this estimate.
- **Variable consideration.** Usage-based fees are variable consideration. The usage-based royalty exception or the "allocate to the distinct period" approach usually means recognizing as usage occurs instead of estimating up front.
- **Ramped pricing.** Escalating annual fees in a multi-year deal may need the transaction price allocated across the term, and discounts on future years may create a material right.
- **Tiered or volume pricing.** Retroactive volume discounts reduce the transaction price and can require a revenue reversal estimate.
- **Billings vs revenue.** Monthly overage billed in arrears creates unbilled receivables. Annual prepaid commits build deferred revenue. Forecast both when cash matters.
